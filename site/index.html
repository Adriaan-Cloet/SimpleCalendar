SimpleCalendar

A lightweight, local-first desktop calendar for Ubuntu and Windows. All your calendars in one quiet window.

What it does

  • Shows your calendars in a day, week and month view, with reminders as desktop notifications.
  • Connects to Google Calendar, CalDAV servers (such as Nextcloud or Radicale) and ICS subscriptions, and keeps calendars of its own on your computer.
  • Lets you create, edit and delete events, also for a single occurrence of a repeating event. Changes made offline are sent once you are back online.

Your data stays with you

SimpleCalendar has no server and no account of its own. Your events are stored on your computer and are only exchanged with the calendar services you connect. Passwords and sign-in tokens are kept in your system's keychain. There is no tracking and no analytics. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Google Calendar

When you add a Google account, SimpleCalendar asks Google for permission to see the list of your calendars and to view and edit their events. It uses that permission only to show your calendars and to save the changes you make in the app.

Get it
