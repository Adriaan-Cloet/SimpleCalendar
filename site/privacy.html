SimpleCalendar

Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026.

SimpleCalendar is a desktop calendar app for Ubuntu and Windows, made by Adriaan Cloet. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and how you can remove it.

In short: SimpleCalendar runs on your computer and has no server of its own. Your calendar data is stored on your computer and is exchanged only with the calendar services you connect yourself. Nobody else receives it, including the developer.

Data the app handles

  • Calendar data: your calendars (name, colour, time zone) and their events (such as title, time, location, description, how they repeat and their reminders).
  • Sign-in details: for Google, an access token and a refresh token; for CalDAV, the server address, your username and your password; for an ICS subscription, its address.
  • Settings: which calendars are shown, their colours, how often they sync and the time zone of the display.

Where it is stored

Calendar data and settings are stored in a database in the app's folder on your computer. Tokens, passwords and subscription addresses are stored in your operating system's keychain (the Secret Service on Ubuntu, the Credential Manager on Windows), never in plain files. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Where it goes

The app connects only to the services you add: Google, the CalDAV servers you sign in to and the addresses of the ICS subscriptions you add. It sends them only what is needed to read your calendars and to save the changes you make. Reminders are shown by your computer; they are not sent anywhere.

SimpleCalendar contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer does not receive any of your data.

Google user data

When you add a Google account, the app asks for two permissions:

  • See the list of your calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly), to show your calendars with their names and colours.
  • View and edit events on all your calendars (calendar.events), to show your events and to save the events you create, change or delete in the app.

The app uses this data only to show your calendars and events to you and to make the changes you ask for. The data is stored on your computer as described above and is not transferred to anyone else. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and no human reads it.

SimpleCalendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Removing an account or subscription in the app (Accounts) deletes its calendars and events from your computer and its sign-in details from the keychain.
  • To withdraw SimpleCalendar's access to your Google account, open Third-party apps and services in your Google account and remove SimpleCalendar.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder removes everything else it stored. Data in your Google, CalDAV or other calendars stays there; the app never deletes it unless you delete an event yourself.

Children

SimpleCalendar is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. The history of every change is public in the source repository.
